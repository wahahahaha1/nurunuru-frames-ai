## Training data of `extension/models/rife-v4.25-nnf.bin`

`rife-v4.25-nnf.bin` is RIFE v4.25 (above) fine-tuned by this project with the tools in `training/`. Besides synthetic frames generated by `training/synth.py`, frames of the following films (end credits excluded, downscaled) were used for training.
